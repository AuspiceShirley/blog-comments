## @auspiceshirley/blog-comments

Welcome to my personal blog!~🍎

You can visit it at [Shirley Auspice's Blog](https://blog.auspiceshirley.dev).

## About This Repository

This repository hosts the comments for my blog, powered by [Giscus](https://github.com/giscus/giscus).  
All comments are stored as GitHub Discussions.

The blog is built with [Astro](https://github.com/withastro/astro) and the [Fuwari](https://github.com/saicaca/fuwari) theme, deployed via [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/).

## House Rules for Comments

By leaving a comment, you agree to follow these three rules:

1. **No political or discriminatory content.**  
   Political propaganda, hate speech, and discrimination based on race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics are strictly prohibited.

2. **No illegal, sensitive, or explicit material.**  
   Do not post anything unlawful, sexually explicit, graphically violent, or otherwise inappropriate. Sharing others' personal information without consent is also forbidden.

3. **No disrespectful behavior, spam, or junk.**  
   Keep discussions respectful and on-topic. Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, advertising, and meaningless noise will not be tolerated.

Comments that violate these rules will be removed without notice.

## How to Comment

- You need a GitHub account to leave a comment.
- Scroll to the bottom of any blog post and use the Giscus widget.
- After one-time authorization, your comment will appear both on the blog and in the [Discussions](https://github.com/auspiceshirley/blog-comments/discussions).

_Feel free to drop a comment – I’d love to hear your thoughts!_
